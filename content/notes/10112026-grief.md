It is such a hard wave that it pulls you heavy to the rough sea, and you do not want to get drowned. You feel you are just breathing, and your body is heavy and filled with ocean waters. There are waves of thoughts that make you grieve about someone. But during the waves of thought, struggling, you can try to be happy and smile at the waves of thought that are not going to stop because it is going to end and give enough time for the rough waves to subside rather than trying so hard to get out of. It is going to be there for all griefs.

Grief can be losing a friendship, losing love, and you are at war within yourself because you do not want to lose.

So take time to grieve; it is going to be there, but it will end up good. There will be hope and light at the end of the tunnel. But once you leave the tunnel, you cannot get back the experience of the tunnel, and it is a bliss looking back at the tunnel.

I thought love can be experienced once, and it is the first love. But that was false; it can be experienced twice.

By always guarding your heart, your heart is getting rusted without oxygen. By opening doors, you let in the oxygen and also the pain of carbon dioxide and other harmful chemicals, but be sure it will always end up good.
